Retail Sales Data Analysis – Oasis Infobyte Internship

1. Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a Retail Sales Dataset to identify sales patterns, customer behaviour, and product category performance.

The main objective is to analyse retail transactions and generate useful business insights using Python.

6. Key Findings

The dataset contains 1,000 records and 9 original columns.

No missing values were found.

The average customer age is approximately 41.39 years.

Electronics generated the highest revenue among the three product categories.

Clothing generated revenue close to Electronics.

Beauty generated comparatively lower revenue.

Price per Unit has a strong positive correlation with Total Amount (0.85).

Monthly sales fluctuated throughout the observed period.

7. Business Recommendations

Introduce promotional campaigns during low-sales periods.

Maintain sufficient inventory for high-revenue categories such as Electronics and Clothing.

Offer product bundles and quantity-based discounts.

Improve Beauty category sales through targeted promotions.

Monitor customer purchasing patterns to improve marketing strategies.

8. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Python and its data analysis libraries can be used to explore retail sales data and identify useful business insights.

The analysis helps understand sales trends, customer demographics, product category performance, and relationships between numerical variables.
